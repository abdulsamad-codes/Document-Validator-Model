B MENT.

The Directorate General of Archaeology and Museums, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa,
established in 1992, is responsible for the protection, preservation and promotion of the
province's cultural heritage. Following the 18th Amendment in 2011, archaeological sites and
museums were also devolved to this Directorate General, which assumed full responsibility for
their management. It oversees archaeological sites, historical monuments and museums across
the province and undertakes excavation, conservation, digitalization and museum management.
The Directorate General also promotes cultural tourism, research and public awareness,
contributing to education and socio-economic development.

The Directorate General of Archaeology and Museums, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

facilitates visitors by allowing video and photography services at archaeological sites and
museums under regulated categories.

These include:

Ordinary documentary filming for students and researchers for academic and
research purposes;

ii. | Commercial documentary and filming for production houses, television channels,
music videos and brand or promotional shoots.

ili. | Wedding photography and videography,

where couples engage photographers or
production teams for personal event cove

rage at designated heritage locations.
The Directorate General of Archaeology and Museums, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
currently collects revenue through a manual ticketing system printed by the Government
Printing Press. Fees for entry, photography and related services are collected in cash at site
counters, recorded manually and maintained in physical registers and basic documentation.

The Directorate General of Archaeology and Museums, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
intends to onboard its services with the FinTech Unit of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Information
Technology Board to enable digital payment solutions. This initiative aims to facilitate secure
and convenient payment options for visitors and Service users, improve transparency in revenue
collection and enhance overall operational efficiency through digitization.
